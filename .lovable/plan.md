# CipherHill production refinement

## Goal
Refine the existing single-page website into a concise, production-ready CipherHill site while preserving its dark navy, teal, amber, typography, imagery, motion, and overall layout language.

## Changes
- Replace every AlpineRed brand reference with CipherHill and use “Cybersecurity & Technology Solutions” as the supporting brand line.
- Simplify the hero to the supplied headline, short supporting copy, two calls to action, and a factual three-item trust strip.
- Rework the services into four concise cards: Software Testing & Security, Application Security, Cloud Security & Migration, and Risk & Compliance.
- Replace the large cloud spotlight with a short AI Security & Automation section so cloud remains one practice rather than the centerpiece.
- Add concise Who We Help and three-step How It Works sections.
- Replace About and Pricing content with the supplied short, claim-safe wording and remove unsupported credentials, statistics, address, phone, and other invented details.
- Simplify the final contact area around “Ready to Secure Your Software?”, retain a usable inquiry form, and use hello@cipherhill.com.
- Rebuild the footer content around the requested Services, Company, Legal, email, and fixed 2026 copyright. Add simple Privacy Policy and Terms of Service sections so those links have valid destinations.
- Update route metadata, structured data, favicon branding, theme storage key, image alt text, navigation, and hidden references to CipherHill and cipherhill.com.

## Production checks
- Search source and public files for old branding, unsupported claims, fake credentials, fake statistics, placeholders, address, and phone number.
- Verify all navigation, calls to action, legal links, theme toggle, mobile menu, and contact form behavior.
- Validate desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts in the live preview.
- Confirm the final page title, metadata, favicon, footer, and current build status.

## Technical details
- Keep the existing TanStack Start route structure and component organization.
- Reuse the current semantic color tokens, responsive grid patterns, Reveal motion, and theme toggle.
- Keep the contact form as a front-end inquiry experience; no data service or email delivery will be added without a connected backend.
